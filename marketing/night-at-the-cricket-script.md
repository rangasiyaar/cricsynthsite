# A Night at the Cricket — trailer script

A 30-second brand trailer built from the poster `posters/png/night-at-the-cricket.png`. It plays like an open-world
game trailer: music-led, slow push-ins on each scene, a freeze and a character title card, then the line.
No product, no numbers, no features. 9:16 for Reels and Shorts; a 16:9 cut uses the same beats.

**Feel:** a warm night, a city that's switched on, a crowd holding its breath. Confident and a little playful, never loud.
**Music:** a slow, heavy beat at about 85 BPM (low bass, soft synth pads, finger snaps). It builds for 20 seconds, cuts to silence for one beat, then lands on the line.
**Type:** Barlow Condensed, heavy italic, white, the poster's pink-to-gold gradient on the second line of the headline.
**Voice:** none. The only words are on screen; it plays complete with the sound off.

## Notes for the edit

- Every freeze is 6–8 frames of motion blur into a hard stop, with a slight colour lift; the title card's white frame
  matches the poster's panel borders.
- Keep each scene to one camera move: a slow push-in or a slow drift. No shake.
- Hold the headline for at least 3 seconds; it's the whole message.
- Safe areas for 9:16: keep text 220 px clear of the bottom and 120 px of the top.
- Cut-downs: **15 s** (beats 1, 2, 6, 8, 9) and a **6 s** bumper (beat 6 into 9).
- Never: real players, team kits or logos, betting words, numbers or features, and no game names, logos or typefaces.
