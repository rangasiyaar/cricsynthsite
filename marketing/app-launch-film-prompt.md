# Prompt for Claude Design: CricSynthesis launch film (keynote style)

> Paste everything below the line into Claude Design and attach the screenshots from `CricSynthesis-app-screens.zip`.

---

## Brief

Design a **product launch film for the CricSynthesis Android app** in the style of a big-tech keynote product reveal:
minimal, cinematic, confident, unhurried. Think dark stage, a single product in light, huge clean type, slow
deliberate camera moves around the phone, and moments of silence before each reveal. Original work: no imitation of any
company's brand, logo, music, voice or exact scenes.

- **Main film: 16:9 (1920×1080), 75–90 seconds.** Also a **9:16 cut, 30 seconds**, and a **6-second bumper**.
- Deliver a storyboard with one frame per shot, camera move, on-screen type, timing and transitions, and export-ready
  key frames.

**The idea:** *Every match, before it happens.* CricSynthesis turns a cricket match into a complete picture before the
first ball — who is likely to win, the scores that could happen, when wickets fall, who stars, how the ground plays,
the fantasy team — and MatchSynth Lab lets fans change the match and watch everything move.

## Look and feel

- **Stage:** near-black `#0B0D0F` with a faint steel gradient (`#1D2D3D` at the edges); one soft key light on the
  phone. Light sections (`#F2F2F3`) only for the analytics montage, as a deliberate "lights up" moment.
- **Phone:** a clean, unbranded modern Android device, dark frame. Screens come from the attached screenshots
  (dark versions on the dark stage). Slow 3D rotation, gentle parallax, depth of field.
- **Type:** **Barlow Condensed** 600 uppercase for statements (very large, tracked slightly wide), **Barlow** for small
  lines, **JetBrains Mono** for numbers. White on dark; one accent at a time: steel `#5980A6`, copper `#B0613A` for the
  second team, green `#2E8C57` only for "Lineup Out".
- **Charts become sculpture:** individual charts lift off the screen and float in 3D space as glass-like panels (16px
  corners, soft shadow), then animate: bars rise, lines trace, ranges expand, heatmaps fill.
- **Pace:** long holds, slow push-ins, cuts on musical accents. Never more than one idea on screen.
- **Never:** betting/gambling words, live-score claims, how the forecasts are made (no simulation counts, no "AI
  model", no data sources), league/team logos.

## Sound

Original score: a single sustained synth note under the opening, a low pulse when the phone appears, building strings
and soft percussion through the analytics montage (each chart lands on a beat), a held breath of silence before the Lab
reveal, then the full theme. Subtle foley: glass panels settling, a soft click on taps, one clean bat-on-ball sound
under the final logo. Optional narration, calm and warm, one line per chapter (the on-screen statement).
