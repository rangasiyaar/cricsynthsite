## Brief

A **vertical 9:16 (1080×1920) reel, 40–45 seconds**, for **CricSynthesis**, an Android app that turns every cricket
match into a full analytics briefing before the first ball: win chances, score ranges, wicket timing, player outlooks,
match-ups, ground intelligence and a fantasy team — plus **MatchSynth Lab**, where fans change the match and see what
moves.

**The star of this reel is the data visualisation.** Every scene shows a real chart from the app, animated as if it is
being drawn live: bars rising, lines tracing, heatmaps filling cell by cell. It must feel like the most detailed match
preview a fan has ever held in their hand.

For Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp status. Works with the **sound off** (all ideas in on-screen text),
hooks in the **first 2 seconds**.

**Goal:** Android downloads. **Call to action:** "Free on Android · cricsynthesis.in/app".

**Deliver:** one storyboard frame per scene (9:16), the motion for each, the on-screen copy and timings, export-ready
frames, plus a **15-second cut** (scenes 1, 3, 5, 8, 12) and a **dark version**.

## Audience and tone

- Cricket fans in India and South Asia, 18–35, who follow T20 leagues, internationals, women's and U19 cricket and play
  fantasy cricket.
- **Confident, precise, a little cinematic.** Short lines. Numbers do the talking.
- **Never:** betting or gambling words ("odds", "bet", "tips", "sure-shot", "guaranteed", "win money"); live-score
  claims; how the forecasts are made (no counts of runs or matches behind them, no "AI model", no method names, no
  data sources); league, board or franchise logos.

## Chart animation rules

- Bars grow from their baseline (ease-out, 0.5–0.7s, 30ms stagger); lines trace left to right; ranges grow from the
  median outward; heatmaps fill in reading order.
- Keep axis labels and numbers legible: at least 1.5s on screen after a chart finishes drawing.
- Team colours always blue (first team) and copper (second team), as in the app.
- One idea per frame; a chart may be enlarged out of the phone, but never redrawn with numbers that aren't in the
  screenshot.

## Text rules

Headlines max 7 words, subs max 9. Safe area: nothing important in the top 220px or bottom 320px. Captions burned in.
Optional voice-over: the headline only, calm and confident.

## Sound

Modern percussive track with a light tabla/dhol texture, 115–122 BPM, cuts on the beat; soft UI taps on interactions;
a short rising tone each time a chart finishes; a bat-on-ball "tock" on the end card. Royalty-free.
