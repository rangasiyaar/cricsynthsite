## Brief

Design a **vertical (9:16, 1080×1920) app overview video reel, 30–35 seconds**, for **CricSynthesis**, an
Android app for cricket fans: win chances, projected scores, playing XIs and fantasy picks before the first ball,
plus MatchSynth Lab, where fans change the match (pitch, dew, toss, a player's form) and see what moves.

Use it for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp status. It must work **with the sound off** (every idea is in
on-screen text) and grab attention in the **first 2 seconds**.

**Goal:** downloads of the Android app. **Call to action:** "Free on Android · cricsynthesis.in/app".

Deliver the scene-by-scene storyboard as frames (one frame per scene, 9:16), with the motion described for each, the
on-screen copy, timings, and an export-ready version of each frame. Also give a 15-second cut (scenes 1, 3, 5, 7, 9).

## Audience and tone

- Indian cricket fans, 18–35, who play fantasy cricket and follow T20 leagues, internationals and women's cricket.
- Tone: **sharp, confident, sporty, analytical**. Short lines. No hype words ("revolutionary", "AI-powered",
  "game-changer"), no emojis in the frames.
- **Never** use betting or gambling language: no "odds", "bet", "tips", "sure-shot", "guaranteed", "win money".
  We sell analytics and projections, not predictions to gamble on. No live scores claims.
- **Never** mention how the model works: no number of simulations, no "AI model", no method names, no data sources.

## Text rules for the frames

- Headlines max 6 words, body max 9 words. Always readable for at least 1.5 seconds.
- Safe area: keep text out of the top 220px and bottom 320px (Reels UI).
- Team names in the screenshots are real fixtures: don't add or change team logos, and don't use any league,
  board or franchise logos or trademarks anywhere.
- Captions burned in; optional voice-over line per scene is the headline read in a calm, confident voice.

## Sound

Upbeat, modern percussion with a light tabla or dhol texture, 110–120 BPM, cuts on the beat; soft UI tap and swipe
sounds on interactions; a short bat-on-ball "tock" on the end card logo. Royalty-free.
